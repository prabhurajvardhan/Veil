# Concept: Privacy Engine

## Definition
The local component that detects sensitive information in the fused perception result and ensures it is sanitized before anything may cross the network boundary.

See `02-system-design/privacy/privacy-engine.md` for full design detail.

## Why It Exists
Directly implements Privacy Principles #1, #2, #6, #7, #8 from the project brief — it is the architectural embodiment of VEIL's core product promise.
